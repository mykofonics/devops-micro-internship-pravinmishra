Question 2 — What is AWS Free Tier, and how long does it last?

The AWS Free Tier is a program offered by Amazon Web Services (AWS) that lets allows new users explore and use many AWS services at no cost, within specified usage limits. It is designed to help you learn, build projects, and experiment with AWS before incurring charges.

AWS Free Tier consists of three categories:
12-Month Free Tier
Available to new AWS customers for 12 months from the date they create their AWS account.

Always Free
These services remain free indefinitely, provided you stay within the monthly limits.

Free Trials
Some AWS services offer free trials for a limited period, such as 30 days, 60 days, or another specified duration.



Question 3 — Name three AWS Free Tier services and their free usage limits.

Amazon EC2: 750 hours/month of eligible instance usage.
Amazon S3: 5 GB of Standard Storage.
Amazon RDS: 750 hours/month of eligible database usage and 20 GB of storage.
